02 / Technical scope

Architecture assessment domains

Workloads & APIs

Inventory deployments, StatefulSets, operators, CRDs and platform-specific APIs; identify portability gaps.

Networking

Review routes, ingress, DNS, certificates, service exposure and network policies.

Security & governance

Map SCC-dependent workloads to compatible security controls; validate RBAC, service accounts and policy enforcement.

Storage & data

Assess CSI drivers, storage classes, access modes, backup/restore and application-consistent data migration.

Delivery & GitOps

Assess manifest and Helm portability, registry access, secrets references and reconciliation boundaries.

Observability & operations

Define health checks, dashboards, alerts, runbooks and operational handover criteria.

03 / Delivery methodology

Illustrative migration lifecycle

Stage 01

Discover

Inventory workloads, dependencies, platform services and business criticality.

Stage 02

Design

Define target architecture, compatibility mappings and acceptance criteria.

Stage 03

Prepare

Establish target prerequisites, access, storage, connectivity and delivery workflows.

Stage 04

Pilot

Validate representative workloads and document compatibility gaps.

Stage 05

Migrate

Plan controlled waves with agreed change windows and rollback criteria.

Stage 06

Validate

Check application health, data integrity, security and operational readiness.

04 / Architecture leadership

Decision and governance framework

Key responsibilities in a platform migration architecture include aligning technical stakeholders, reviewing compatibility and dependencies, defining migration boundaries, documenting architectural decisions, agreeing acceptance gates and coordinating operational readiness. These are role-oriented considerations rather than a verified record of individual project responsibilities.

05 / Acceptance & resilience

Validation and cutover criteria

  • Workload deployment, readiness and application functional checks.
  • Service discovery, ingress, DNS and TLS validation.
  • Persistent data integrity and recovery readiness.
  • Access controls, workload security and governance checks.
  • Monitoring, alerting, logging and operational handover.
  • Agreed rollback triggers, recovery procedure and accountable owners.

Actual migration results, scale, timelines and quantified improvements are not published.
